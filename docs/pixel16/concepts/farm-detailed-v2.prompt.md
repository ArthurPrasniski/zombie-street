# Fazenda detalhada v2 — conceito visual

Modo: ferramenta integrada `image_gen` via skill `imagegen`.
Entradas: as duas imagens fornecidas pelo usuário nesta conversa, como referências
exclusivamente de linguagem visual e acabamento. Nenhum asset externo baixado.
Destino: conceito de direção artística; não é cenário limpo ou sprite pronto para runtime.

## Prompt enviado

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: visual target concept for the portrait mobile game Zombie Road. Generate a NEW detailed pixel art image using the TWO USER ATTACHED IMAGES as STYLE REFERENCES ONLY (not edit targets). Match their sophisticated handcrafted pixel clusters, rich material textures, selective outlines, warm localized light, ambient shadows, top-down three-quarter orthographic RPG camera and dense environmental storytelling. The previous simple procedural pixel prototype is NOT the desired style. Scene: a rural zombie defense arena, an abandoned Brazilian farm at late afternoon, portrait 2:3 composition. Entire playable rectangular arena visible, no phone mockup, no text, no UI, no black vignette. North/top: weathered red barn and broken wood fencing, scattered farm crates and tools along perimeter. Left and right margins: layered trees and crops, aged wood, brick patches, barrels, small farming equipment with readable individual shapes, worn surfaces and precise pixel highlights. Center: broad unobstructed dirt-and-grass battle corridor, subdued texture and sparse patches, usable open deployment area, no furniture blocking it. Bottom 13 percent: horizontal sandbag defense line and a recognizable weathered orange pickup truck with mounted defensive gun beneath it, detailed windshield, tires and metal reflections, facing north. Populate sparingly: a brown-haired female green-clad rifle defender near lower third, one sheriff and dog, a few recognizable zombies descending from upper third. Characters must be small relative to the arena (around 6 to 8 percent of image height), detailed RPG sprites, compact believable proportions rather than giant bobble heads. Lighting: convincing consistent warm sunlight, a few warm barn lamps, restrained cool ambient shadows, hand-placed pixel light pools. Palette: earthy greens, terracotta, warm ochre, dark plum outlines, controlled saturation. Crisp deliberate pixel art with the same level of material richness and object detail as the supplied references. No smooth vector shapes, no crude block placeholders, no 3D render, no photorealism, no blur, no grain overlay, no enormous characters. This is a concept establishing the new art direction, not an animation sheet or final runtime asset.
```
